## Objective

Deliver the OzempicAI product strategy on the current React PWA and Supabase stack. Repair core logging and planning first, then validate an Appetite Compass for adults managing changing appetite, including prescribed GLP-1 users.

## Program scope

- Product discovery and retention instrumentation
- Reliable health-data entry, editable goals, and correction
- My Day and reusable meals
- Appetite Compass and reviewed meal recommendations
- Flexible Week, Rescue Meal, and grocery reconciliation
- Meal Memory with text, barcode, and photo-assisted capture
- Strength and daily-life progress
- Weekly Story and a user-reviewed visit brief
- Gentle return and maintenance preferences
- Offline reliability, privacy, access control, rollout, and measurement

## Delivery gates

- **Week 2:** validate the target customer job and establish a retention baseline; if logging friction dominates, move logging ahead of personalization.
- **Week 6:** require reliable saves, usable planning, and relevant curated recommendations before expanding assisted capture.
- **Week 12:** expand only when mature retention evidence supports the investment and trust, reliability, and cost remain acceptable.

## Primary outcome

W4 useful retention: the percentage of randomized eligible accounts completing a confirmed meal log or marking a planned meal used on at least two distinct local days during days 22–28 after assignment. Include every assigned account in the denominator.

## Completion criteria

- [ ] All implementation issues are complete or explicitly descoped at a documented gate
- [ ] Manual logging remains available when recommendation or provider services fail
- [ ] No unresolved critical privacy, clinical-safety, or cross-account access defects
- [ ] The rollout decision uses the predeclared W4 metric and a stable holdout
- [ ] Business retention is evaluated separately using verified billing events
